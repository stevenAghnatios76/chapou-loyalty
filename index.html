Since day one

It's more than just a sea view.

Chapouu started as a small stand on the Boulevard with a grill, a fridge full of fruit and a view that did most of the selling. The view is still there. So is the grill. What we added is everything worth staying for — long tables, no rush, and food that tastes like someone actually cared how it was put together.
